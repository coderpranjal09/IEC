About IEC

Building a Culture of Innovation

IEC encourages students to think beyond conventional boundaries, develop entrepreneurial skills, explore innovative technologies, and transform their ideas into real-world solutions.

As the official Innovation & Entrepreneurship Cell of THDC Institute of Hydropower Engineering and Technology, Tehri, we serve as a bridge between academic learning and real-world entrepreneurial impact.

Mission

Foster a culture of innovation and entrepreneurship among students through mentorship, resources, and hands-on experience.

Vision

To become a leading hub of student-driven innovation and startup creation in Uttarakhand and beyond.

What We Do

Our Innovation Ecosystem

Six pillars that support student innovators, budding entrepreneurs, and future changemakers at every stage of their journey.

Entrepreneurship Development

Workshops, bootcamps, and resources to build entrepreneurial mindset and business fundamentals.

Startup Incubation

Support for early-stage startups with mentorship, workspace, and access to funding opportunities.

Innovation & Ideation

Ideation sessions, design thinking workshops, and creative problem-solving sprints for real challenges.

Hackathons & Competitions

Regular hackathons, innovation challenges, and competitions to test and refine student ideas.

Industry Collaboration

Partnerships with industry leaders, startups, and experts to bridge academic and industry gaps.

Mentorship & Networking

Connect with experienced mentors, alumni entrepreneurs, and a community of like-minded innovators.

Startup Spotlight

Sample Project — HydroSense

HydroSense is a student-led innovation project developing low-cost IoT sensors for real-time water quality monitoring in hydropower-adjacent communities. The project aims to provide actionable data for environmental conservation and sustainable water management.
